## Substitutions and deviations from the design

* Floor 90 Blue: class CHAMPION (Emerald's), not CHAMPION_FRLG: battle_message.c prints the rival's name for RIVAL_EARLY_FRLG, RIVAL_LATE_FRLG and CHAMPION_FRLG alike, so only a non-FRLG class keeps the name BLUE.
* Floor 90 Blue: the Mega is Alakazam (Alakazite, the design allows Pidgeot or Alakazam); Pidgeot leads without a stone (one Mega per trainer).
* Floor 100 Red: Charizard holds Charizardite X (the Drought of Mega Y would weaken Red's own Blastoise and Lapras and Leaf's Duo Blastoise).
* Floor 100 Leaf: class RIVAL (prints 'PKMN TRAINER LEAF', the house data's own class for LEAF); her Mega is Blastoise (Blastoisinite).
* Floor 70 Steven: class CHAMPION as in the design table (Emerald's own trainer uses RIVAL, 'PKMN TRAINER').
* Floor 10 Brock: Kabuto knows only three moves at level 10 (Scratch, Absorb, Harden are all its level-up moves).
* Floor 75 Brandon: the three strong members are Garchomp, Excadrill and Mega Tyranitar, a sandstorm team around the Regis (Tyranitar's Sand Stream boosts Regirock's Sp. Def and Excadrill's Sand Rush).
* Floor 75 Lucy and Brandon use the house encounter music of src/data/trainers.party (Male) for the Frontier Brains.
* Moves come from each species' own learnsets as the game dumps them (level-up at or below the level; from floor 30 its TM/tutor list; from 50 its line's egg moves). A move a pre-evolution learns but the species itself does not is never used (e.g. Breloom has no Spore), so every set is legal without tracing when a Pokemon evolved.
* An item evolution that stands in for a trade (the Linking Cord, or the item of the same pair's trade evolution, such as Onix's Metal Coat) counts as a trade evolution (from level 30).
* Judgement exclusions (movedex.py): status moves without a use for the AI in a single battle (Splash, Celebrate, Hold Hands, Transform, Sketch, the doubles-only and ally-only moves), evasion boosts, accuracy drops from floor 20, and damaging moves whose effect a trainer cannot set up (Return, Frustration, Hidden Power, Focus Punch, Counter, Mirror Coat, Present, Natural Gift, Belch, Snore, Dream Eater...), and on floors 1-9 damaging moves above 60 power (the later generations' learnsets give some basic Pokemon strong moves at level 1, such as Snubbull's three elemental Fangs). 'Four moves unless fewer are legal' counts the moves left after these exclusions.
* Species never used on regular floors: Ditto, Smeargle, Unown, Wobbuffet/Wynaut, Shedinja, Magikarp, the cocoon stages, Feebas, Happiny, Burmy, Combee, Tyrogue, Kricketot, and every species that changes form in battle other than by Mega Evolution (Castform, Cherrim, Darmanitan, Aegislash, Meloetta).
* Regular trainers use Emerald's own trainer classes and pictures; their region is Hoenn. The Kanto bosses' floors (10, 20, 25, 90, 100) are Kanto and their Duo partners use FRLG classes and pictures.
* Duo trainers carry no extra AI flag: battle_ai_main.c adds AI_FLAG_DOUBLE_BATTLE to every AI in a double battle whose flags are not zero, and every Tower trainer has flags.
* The strongest AI set (bosses 70, 80, 90 and, with Ace Pokemon, the specials and the final) is Smart Trainer + Prediction + HP Aware.
* Field texts are plain ASCII: the classes the game prints with a glyph are written POKEFAN, POKEMANIAC, PKMN RANGER, PKMN BREEDER and SWIMMER in the intro lines, and POKEMON stands for the game's POKéMON.
