Battle FX data for the game's 3D battles (include/diorama/battle_fx.h).
Packed by tools/battlefx/pack_data.py from the Battle FX integration package's native/ folder (Battle FX v3.3: battlefx_kit commit 9848e06, tag lab-perf-v3.3).
The textures and meshes are procedural (no game or Nintendo art); the animation data is converted from
pokeemerald-expansion's own battle_anim_scripts.s and C sources (commit in bfx_moves.json meta.commit: 50351f314162faf2301c2a4151ffc5080e51e7db, this game's tree); by tools/battlefx/convert_data.py (the package's converter, keeping the panse commands' pan step and delay and the tasks' priorities, a ';' ending a statement).
